**Focus:** CoP decomposes jailbreak attacks into reusable principles — framing, authority, distraction, escalation — and uses an agentic system to compose these principles into structured multi-stage attack sequences. By systematically exploring the composition space, CoP discovers attack pathways that single-principle baselines miss.

## Key Insights

- **Principle composition as the unit of attack**: Rather than treating jailbreak prompts as monolithic artefacts, CoP treats them as compositions of atomic principles. This enables systematic search and reuse — a successful framing principle from a chemistry jailbreak can be combined with an escalation principle from a cybersecurity jailbreak.
- **Scalable discovery**: The compositional space grows exponentially with the number of principles, and CoP uses a guided search rather than brute force — a significant efficiency gain over genetic or evolutionary search approaches.
- **Novel risk discovery**: CoP surfaces risks that do not appear in benchmark datasets because they require specific principle combinations that no human red-teamer anticipated — a reminder that coverage-based evaluation systematically misses out-of-distribution attacks.

## Failure-First Relevance

CoP's compositional approach directly parallels the Failure-First operator design philosophy: operators are content-agnostic transforms that compose framing, authority, and obfuscation mechanisms. The principle library CoP develops is a concrete implementation of what the Failure-First design calls "the machine" in the Empty Factory rule — a reusable, composable attack mechanism that can be applied to any seed.
